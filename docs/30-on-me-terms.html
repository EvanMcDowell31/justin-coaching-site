30 on Me Terms

Last updated: November 19, 2026

In plain words, here is how the free first 30 works. Booking First 30 on Me means you accept these terms.

1. The offer

  • Your first 30 minute session with me, live on Zoom, is free. Book the First 30 on Me session; no code or card is needed. The normal price is $75.
  • This offer is for first time actors working with me. One free session per actor, subject to availability.
  • All 30 minutes are a real working session on your scene. There is no obligation to continue.
  • If we want to keep working past 30 minutes, I will ask first. Extra time is charged at my normal rates only if you agree.

2. The review (optional)

  • Afterward, I may ask for an honest review. It is optional, and saying no does not change your free session in any way.
  • Honest means honest. Say what helped and what did not. I am not asking for a script or a five star rating.

3. Using a review in marketing

  • I will ask you separately, in writing, before using your name, image, voice or any part of your review in marketing. Nothing is used without that separate yes.
  • If you say yes, it may appear on justindavidcoaching.com, my social media, my email newsletter, and ads for my coaching.
  • I may edit it for length, add captions, and use short clips, stills or written quotes. I will not change your words to say something you did not say.
  • I decide which parts, if any, get used, and I will let you know before anything goes live.
  • Giving permission is your choice and is never a condition of the free session. There is no payment or royalty for it.

4. Privacy, minors and changing your mind

  • Your sides, scripts, casting notes and the takes you record stay confidential and are never shared, as described in my Privacy Policy.
  • Actors under 18 need a parent or guardian to arrange the booking, be present for the session, and give any permission on their behalf.
  • You can ask me to stop using your review at any time by emailing justin@justindavidcoaching.com. I will remove it from my website and social media within 14 days. I cannot pull back ads already running or copies others have already shared.

5. Agreeing

  • Booking First 30 on Me means you accept these terms. Permission to use a review is always asked for separately.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Georgia, and my Terms of Service also apply.
  • Questions first are always welcome at justin@justindavidcoaching.com.
