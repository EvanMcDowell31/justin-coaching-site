Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Justin David Coaching (“I,” “me,” or “my”) operates www.justindavidcoaching.com and offers self tape reader sessions and acting coaching over Zoom. This policy explains what information I collect when you visit the site, book a session, or get in touch, how I use it, and the choices you have. By using the site or booking a session, you agree to this policy.

1. Information I collect

  • Information you give me: your name, email address and phone number when you book a session, plus anything you share in my contact form or coaching questionnaire, such as where you are in your career, whether you have representation, and your goals.
  • Session materials: audition sides, scripts, casting notes and any other materials you send so we can prepare for a session.
  • Payment information: payments are handled by my booking provider and its payment processor. I never see or store your full card number.
  • Information collected automatically: when you visit the site, your browser sends information such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you visit, how long you spend on them, how you arrived at the site, and how you scroll and click.

2. How I use your information

  • To schedule and run your sessions, including confirmations, reminders and Zoom links.
  • To reply to your messages and questionnaire, and to prepare for your consultation or coaching.
  • To understand how the site is used so I can improve it and my services.
  • To measure my advertising and show my ads to people who have visited the site.
  • To meet legal, tax and record keeping obligations.

I do not sell your personal information.

3. Cookies, analytics and advertising

This site uses cookies, pixels, tags, scripts and similar technologies, including those provided by third party analytics and advertising partners. These technologies may automatically collect information about your device and your activity on the site, such as the pages you view, the links you click, how you scroll and move through pages, and whether you book a session.

Specifically, this site uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the site, and the Meta Pixel to measure my advertising. To learn how Google uses data when you use sites that use its services, visit policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your visits and interactions may be monitored and recorded in aggregated or session level form to help me understand how the site is used, measure the performance of my marketing, improve the site and my services, and show relevant ads to people who have previously visited the site, including on other websites and platforms.

Third party partners may combine this information with other information they have collected about you, including through your signed in accounts with them, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, including blocking or deleting them, and you can limit personalized advertising through your device settings and industry opt out tools such as optout.aboutads.info. Blocking cookies may cause some parts of the site to not work as intended.

4. Who I share information with

I share information only with trusted service providers that help me operate my business, such as scheduling and payment processing, video conferencing, website hosting, form handling, video hosting, analytics and advertising. These providers may only use your information to perform services on my behalf.

I may also disclose information if required by law, to respond to legal requests, or to protect my rights, property or safety, or that of others.

5. Your sessions and materials

Your sides, scripts and casting materials are treated as confidential. I won’t share them, post them, or use them for anything other than your session.

I don’t record our Zoom sessions unless we agree to it beforehand. Any self tape footage you record is yours.

6. How long I keep information

I keep your information only as long as needed to provide my services, keep accurate business records, and meet legal obligations. You can ask me to delete it at any time (see below).

7. Security

I use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of sending or storing data over the internet is completely secure, and I can’t guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask me to access, correct or delete the personal information I hold about you, or to stop sending you emails, by contacting me at the address below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights, and I’ll honor them as required by law.

9. Text messages

If you opt in to text messages, such as appointment reminders, message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. I do not sell or share your phone number or text message opt in information with third parties for their marketing.

10. Younger actors

This site is not directed to children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. Actors under 18 should book sessions with the consent and involvement of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child under 13 has given me personal information, please contact me and I’ll delete it.

11. Links to other sites

This site links to other websites, such as my booking page, IMDb and social media. I’m not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please review their privacy policies.

12. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when they’re posted on this page, and the date at the top will show when it was last updated.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy or your information? Email me at justin@justindavidcoaching.com or use the contact page on this site.
