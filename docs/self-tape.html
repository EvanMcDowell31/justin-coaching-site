Scene 05 · Honest expectations

Who I may not be the right fit for

01

You're expecting every audition to be approached the same way

A one line audition and a six page dramatic scene don’t require the same preparation. I prefer to work around what the material actually needs rather than follow a rigid formula.

02

You’re looking for a one size fits all acting method.

I didn’t come into this industry with a theater degree or years of formal training. I had to figure out what worked for me through experience, trial, error, and a lot of learning. I respect that everyone’s process is different.

03 · FOR 1:1 COACHING CLIENTS

You’re expecting me to get you an agent or book you a role.

I can’t promise either of those things. What I can do is share what I’ve learned about building your materials, preparing for auditions, finding opportunities, and presenting yourself professionally. Simply put, I can show you the way and how to get there and guide you. I can be by your side as much as I can. But I can't do the work for you. And there is no minimum commitment for coaching. You stay because the work is helping you, and you can stop whenever it stops serving you.

04

You're more focused on delivering lines than listening.

I’m not against traditional techniques. I just believe there’s value in finding practical approaches that work for you, especially when you have limited time to prepare. Not every audition needs a 45 minute emotional excavation.

05

You're looking for someone to create an entire performance for you

I'll help you work through the material, but I still want the choices and personality to come from you. The goal isn't to make you act like me. It's to help you bring more of yourself to the work.

06

If you are looking for a guaranteed booking

I can't promise you'll book the role. What I can do is help you prepare, make stronger choices, and put together an audition you're confident submitting.

Scene 06 · Your questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does a self tape session cost?

Sessions are $75 for 30 minutes, $150 for 60 minutes, or $200 for 2 hours. There's no package or ongoing commitment. You book the time you need for the audition in front of you.

Which session should I book for my first time?

I usually recommend the 60 minute session for first time clients. It gives us time to work the scene and still get it on tape without rushing.

Do I need a professional self tape setup?

No. You need to be well lit from the front, have a background that isn't distracting, and record clean audio. If you want help with lighting, camera, or background, let me know when you book.

Final take

Whenever you are ready, I am here

If a tape is due today, we can take it step by step. If it is due next week, we can prepare with the same care. Either way, you will be met with patience, honesty, and respect for the work you are doing.
